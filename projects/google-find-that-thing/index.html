google FIND THAT THING

For Google Lens, I created the product visuals that sit inside the supplied “Find That Thing” animation. I adapted the idea across different objects, formats and social placements, building a playful visual rhythm that shows how quickly a real-world image can become a search. My focus was keeping every version fresh while making the Lens interaction immediately understandable.
